# AgentOS Product Overview

## Approved capabilities

AgentOS is the organizational intelligence foundation approved for the September launch. It indexes organizational documents into source-cited evidence, supports hybrid retrieval across semantic vectors, PostgreSQL full-text search, metadata, and the knowledge graph, and records each retrieval execution in shared memory.

The AI Team owns the retrieval platform. The platform uses PostgreSQL with pgvector and Gemini embeddings. It preserves chunk, page, section, and document citations.

## Product direction

The product is designed for CTOs and operations leaders who need traceable organizational answers. Generated answers must be grounded in retrieved evidence; unsupported claims must be withheld.
