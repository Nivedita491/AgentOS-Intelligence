# AgentOS Pricing Strategy

## Approved pricing

On 2026-07-28, the Pricing Council approved the AgentOS Pro plan at ₹2,999 per month for up to 25 users. The Starter plan remains ₹999 per month for up to 5 users. Enterprise pricing requires a sales quote and is not approved for public numeric claims.

## Approval

Maya Shah approved the public pricing language after review with Finance. The Growth Team may use the Pro and Starter prices only when paired with their user limits.
